Calibration Marker

Open a frame, pick a point in the list, then click it on the frame. Scroll to zoom, right-drag (or Shift-drag) to pan, ⌘/Ctrl-Z to undo. To move a mark, select it and click again. Mark every point you can see clearly; skip hidden ones. Needs at least 5 floor points spread across the field and 4 HUB rim corners.
